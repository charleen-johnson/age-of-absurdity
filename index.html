A social commentary on the era we are living in.

Enter the Field Read the Essays Field Paper 02 ↗
Out beyond ideas of wrongdoing and rightdoing, there is a field. I'll meet you there. Rumi, trans. Coleman Barks

The orientation field is alive and co-creative. Interact with it.

We are living in an era where the maps no longer match the terrain. The frameworks, the models, the ways of navigating work and life and institutions that were handed down to us are losing their grip on what is actually happening. The complexity is deeper. The contradictions are more relentless. The pace does not allow for sense-making.

Absurdity is what surfaces in that gap. Not dysfunction. Not failure. Not a verdict on anyone's intelligence or effort. A signal. The system telling you something true about the conditions you are actually in, if you know how to read it.

This series reads those signals. It is a social commentary on the era we are living in, grounded in research, written for anyone who has felt the fracture and wondered what it means.

Absurdity is what surfaces when a system loses contact with what is actually happening. Not dysfunction. Not stupidity. A signal. The meeting that went nowhere. The strategy that made perfect sense and produced confusion. These are not failures of effort. They are the system telling you something true.

Absurdity is a neutral signal. What is not neutral is what happens next. Does the signal travel? What's the ripple effect?

The Author

Charleen Johnson

Founder, InSpark Group & Behaving Badly HQ

Researcher. Writer. Practitioner. Someone who spent years handing people frameworks and maps, believing in them, and eventually had to reckon with the fact that the maps were failing.

It took losing her parents, a near-fatal diagnosis of something so rare only three hundred people in the world had ever had it, and then a global pandemic arriving while she was still recovering, to finally see it clearly. She was complicit. She was part of it. This work comes from that reckoning.

She is the founder of InSpark Group and creator of the Organizational Absurdity Research Program. Author of Who Are My Muffin (BBHQ Books, 2025) and the Field Paper series. The Age of Absurdity is her next book. She writes from the field, not about it.
