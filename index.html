What This Is

This series is a book being built in public. Each essay is a chapter in embryo — an argument developed in the open, tested against real conditions, refined through the process of being read.

The book is called The Age of Absurdity. It argues that what organisations, institutions, and individuals are experiencing right now is not dysfunction or poor leadership. It is something more specific: a progressive loss of the capacity to stay in accurate contact with what is actually happening.

Absurdity is the signal that this capacity has slipped below threshold. It is not what people do wrong. It is what systems produce when their orientation field is under strain.

The research foundation is the Organizational Absurdity Research Program at InSpark Group. The scholarly argument lives in Field Paper 02. These essays are its public voice.

Charleen Johnson

Founder  ·  InSpark Group. & Behaving Badly HQ

Charleen Johnson is the founder of InSpark Group. and creator of the Organizational Absurdity Research Program. Her research sits at the intersection of complexity thinking, sensemaking, and the conditions through which organisations lose — and can recover — their capacity to stay in contact with what they are actually encountering.

She is the author of Who Ate My Muffin (BBHQ Books, 2025) and the Field Paper series. The Age of Absurdity is her next book.
