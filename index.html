The Argument

The maps no longer match the terrain.

We are living in an era where the frameworks, models, roles, institutions, and inherited ways of navigating work and life are losing their grip on what is actually happening. The complexity is deeper. The contradictions are more relentless. The pace often does not allow for sense-making.

Absurdity is what surfaces in that gap. It is the meeting that goes nowhere, the strategy that makes perfect sense and still produces confusion, the institution that keeps performing coherence while everyone quietly senses the drift. These are signals from the field, if we know how to read them.

This project reads those signals. It began in organizations because workplaces make absurdity unusually visible, but the pattern is larger than work. This is a social commentary on the era we are living in, grounded in research, written for anyone who has felt the fracture and wondered what it means.

Absurdity is what appears when a system continues operating from a picture of reality that no longer fits what it is encountering.

Absurdity is a neutral signal. What is not neutral is what happens next. Does the signal travel? What does it reveal?

The Author

Charleen Johnson

Charleen Johnson is a researcher, writer, and organizational transformation consultant. She is the founder of InSpark Group Inc. and creator of the Organizational Absurdity Research Program.

Her work examines why intelligent people and well-intentioned systems so often produce outcomes that feel confusing, performative, contradictory, or strangely detached from reality. She writes at the intersection of organizational complexity, systems thinking, satire, and the lived experience of navigating the age we are actually in.
