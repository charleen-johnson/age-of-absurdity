Essay One

The Orientation Crisis

Why the Age of Absurdity begins with losing our bearings

We have never had more information about the world, and yet it has rarely felt harder to know what is actually going on.

Everywhere we turn, someone is explaining something. There are dashboards, forecasts, feeds, expert takes, leadership models, AI summaries, economic predictions, institutional statements, cultural diagnoses, wellness protocols, strategy decks, political certainties, and more frameworks than any reasonable mammal should be asked to metabolize before lunch. We are surrounded by signals, commentary, interpretation, and advice. The age does not suffer from a shortage of content. If anything, we are drowning in interpretation while feeling strangely less oriented by it.
